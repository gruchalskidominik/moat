Privacy Policy

Moat for iOS · Last updated: September 15, 2026

Moat is built to collect nothing. This page explains exactly what that means.

What Moat does

Moat lets you choose apps, app categories and websites and blocks them on a schedule you set, using Apple's Screen Time frameworks (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings and DeviceActivity).

Data we collect

None that identifies you. Moat has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no crash reporting. The only network request the app ever makes is the optional guardian heartbeat described below.

Guardian mode (optional)

If you choose a guardian, Moat creates a random secret that lives on your phone and in the link you send your guardian. From then on the app periodically sends a small “heartbeat” to a database we host in the EU (Supabase): a random identifier derived from that secret, a timestamp, whether Screen Time access is still granted, and the app version. That is all — no name, no email, no device identifier, no app usage. Your guardian's page reads the same record to show whether Moat is still standing on your phone. Removing the guardian in Settings stops the heartbeats; records are pruned after 90 days.

Screen Time data

When you grant Screen Time access, Apple's frameworks let Moat apply blocks to the apps you selected. Apple deliberately withholds the identity of those apps and any usage data from Moat: the selection is handled by the operating system as opaque tokens. Moat never sees which apps you use, how long, or how often. Your selection and schedules are stored only on your device, in the app's private container.

Camera

If you turn on “Mirror during breath” in Settings, Moat shows a live preview from the front camera during the countdown. The preview is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere, and the camera is off the rest of the time.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Moat sends a local notification only when you tap “Unlock in Moat” on a blocked-app screen. No push notification service is used.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Moat verifies entitlements on device using StoreKit and does not receive your name, email, or payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

Children

Moat is intended for users aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting Moat from your device removes all its data. You can also revoke Screen Time access at any time in Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access.
